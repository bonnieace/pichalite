## One-minute demonstration

1. Enable airplane mode. Open Gallery and select several JPEG/PNG photos.
2. Share them to **PichaLite**, and show that they appear in its queue.
3. Pause the queue while some photos are still waiting.
4. Close PichaLite. For a stronger test, force-stop it in Android Settings.
5. Reopen PichaLite. Show that the photos and paused state remain.
6. Resume processing. Show actual before/after sizes and finished photos.
7. Share a finished photo using Android's Share Sheet.
8. If notifications are allowed, show a completion notification too.
