# Bundled queue animations

The original photo_queue.json animation has been replaced with three independently sourced Lottie animations, bundled for offline playback. JSON is extracted unchanged from the authors' public dotLottie archives; no external image assets or fonts are required.

| Resource | Animation | Creator | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `photo_upload.json` | Upload/Import | Hari | https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/uploadimport-UWv32HdvbL |
